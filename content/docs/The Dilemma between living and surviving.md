# Difference between **Living** and **Surviving**

If you talk about the simple difference between both the words, 'living' means to be alive, grow and be in an active pursuit of joy and purpose, where as 'surviving' refers to fulfilling basic needs in order to live.

However when you apply these words in regards to our day-to-do lives, in my intended perspective, 'living' signifies the act of enjoying your life to the fullest and to LIVE in the moment. On the other hand, 'surviving' anchors your mind to the future, rendering you unable to be carefree and constantly stress over your actions.

# The Dilemma

This dilemma that I speak of is not something that can ever go away from our lives. In each moment and plan, you'll always be struck by this question of whether the mentality I'm taking is right or wrong.

### An Example from my Life
Let's take an example: suppose I'm in the middle of my summer vacation, with a lot of time to kill and even more time to invest. There is a part of me that regrets not utilizing the time I've already spent, and another part of me that feels like failing to use the remainder will make the entire break feel like a total waste. But in the midst of all this frustration, I realize I always wanted to go on a trip to relax and get a break from literally everything. A partial break from my parents, university work, projects, or even my relationship.

If I decide to go on the trip, part of my mind stays behind. I'm physically relaxing, but mentally I'm cursing myself in my head, full of regret and guilt for "wasting time."

On the flip side, if I stay locked in my room grinding away, I check off the boxes. I secure the productivity I starved for. But when I look back, I realize I traded away the only two months I had to take a proper rest, missing out on the entertainment I craved and the memorable trip I wanted all along.

Therefore, the ultimate question stares me right in the face: **Should I work, or should I enjoy?**

# The Thing I Learnt till Now

See, I'm not the best person to give one big answer and solve the dilemma right here. I too suffer from it, and have for the longest time. Countless people have pointed out how volatile I am in my decision-making. When I want to truly begin working on something, I will give it my best; when I don't feel like it, I drop it instantly.

This mentality is definitely not recommended for anyone for the sake of your mental health. Any and everybody will only ever tell you to be balanced and act like a monk.

Perhaps I try to do that to some extent, too. BUT, the one thing I have learnt till now on the basis of my personality is this: sometimes, a little bit of quirkiness and instability is exactly what you need in life. It is definitely better to keep balance between seriousness and being aloof, but sometimes you need to go with what your heart wants and not what your brain thinks is right.

If I want to work today because I had multiple days of unproductivity, I'll simply go and work. In a similar way, regardless of whether everything feels too important to skip, if I really want to rest and take some time off, I won't even think about it. And yes, sometimes I feel bad about resting, but the worst thing you can do is ruin that resting phase with thoughts of the future.

And this is not only about work. There has to be a fine line between what you want to do at any given instance. whether you want to live or survive depends on what your heart wants.
But it should always be within the constraints of your environment. Just because you want luxury don't just go ahead and rob a bank 😭.
# Ending Note

This article is not completely about expressing this dilemma only in terms of life choices, but rather about accepting the chaos of our own minds. We spend so much energy trying to be perfect. Balancing every hour, planning every tomorrow, and eliminating every ounce of guilt. But maybe the secret isn't finding a permanent, unbreakable balance. Maybe it's about giving yourself permission to lean into whatever state you're in. Whether that means locking in and working through the night, or dropping everything to rest, without letting anxiety kill you.

At the end of the day, living isn't about getting the formula right. It's about making peace with the fact that you're figuring it out as you go.
