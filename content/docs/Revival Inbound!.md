# Starting Over (Again)

Sometimes in life you get so frustrated that you want to just leave all the imperfections behind and start anew. However, life is not generous enough to allow you to leave everything behind. So what do you do? You choose to make a change. One can think of adopting a new hairstyle, or getting a new closet, maybe coming out of the closet (I'M KIDDING), or just maybe starting a blog. I thought that picking up something new would distract me enough to survive that frustrating phase I was talking about. Unfortunately for me, I am prone to dropping those things as soon as I pick them when I realize they are too heavy for me to hold.

The truth is that I just never care deeply for these new changes because deep down all I really want is the sensation of change. And as soon as I get that, yup, I go back to living a monotonous life because that's the easiest thing to do. I have survived that way for most of my life. But, that's definitely not the way I want to continue living. Therefore, the one permanent change that I'm going to make is picking up those incomplete projects that helped me survive up until now. 

## Originality

I had been feeling lately that there is nothing unique about myself. I look at countless people around me. Some make a particular sport their personality, some do it with art, and some take something as basic as reading a book look hella cool. I have always envied them. I even went out of the way to ask them how they gained this trait I admire so much. But, almost always, the answer was that they never really thought of it as something that defined them. From their perspective, that thing was just something they had FUN doing and never thought about dropping it. 

Now when I look at myself, I wouldn't go ahead and say that I am a bland person. I have always been curious and experimental. I learn and adapt quickly. I have played almost all types of sports and somehow always excelled at them. I was so quirky that when I was a child I used to take Taekwondo and abacus classes side-by-side. I have played chess at a school National level too. I think I can most definitely call myself a jack of all trades.

But?

Somehow when I look at myself, all I see is an uncertain ball of energy that never masters anything. I think in the midst of switching to different micro-hobbies, I lose that originality I starve for.

Does it mean that I will pick up taekwondo and abacus just to make myself believe that I am more civilized and original? No. I believe that my originality will come from doing things I have fun doing and not dropping them after I get busy. Just like writing a blog, or reading mangas, or something as silly as doodling.

In my head, these small tasks I enjoy doing won't define me as a person but they will definitely make me feel **unique**. And so will working on this blog that I have fun with.
